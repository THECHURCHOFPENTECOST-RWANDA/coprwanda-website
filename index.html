ABOUT OUR CHURCH

The church of pentecost International ,Rwanda is indeed a direct branch of the a major Pentecostal Christian denomination which is an international Church with Headquarters in Accra Ghana HISTORY AND ORGIN WORLDWIDE.: [ .Founding : Started in 1937 throught the missionary work of Irish pastor James McKeown under British Apostolic Church in GoldCoast(Ghana). Name Changes: Founded as the Gold Coast Apostolic Church in 1957,rensmed the Ghana Apostolic Church in 1957 after independence,and formally addopted its current name in August 1962]. .HISTORY OF THE CHURCH OF PENTECOST IN RWANDA: [on 12 August 2000, A Ghanaian Pastor Samuel Yaw Antwi who was a missionary and National Head of the Church in Zimbabwe visited Rwanda together with brother Kalambi Foustin a congolese who was staying in burundi and they welcomed by Brother Jean marie and other six people whom were home cell group members they were brother Khakonge Moise,Brother Marcelin Rachid,Brother Kabiona Daniel,brother Gihimbare Janvier,brother Nkundabagenzi Justin and Inamahoro francine. then Kalambi Faustin introduced Jean marie Wedundu to Ps Samuel Yaw Antwi as a possible leader for new Church to be Started.They were thirty-six members in the cell group.]
